RRevision: LiPo board, all parts on the top face

Status: plan for your review (no board generated yet)This replaces the "everything on the inside face" layout in sections 01–02 wherever they differ. The circuit, LiPo, charger, speaker, antenna network and jar stay the same. The 3×AAA variant is paused on branch aaa-battery.

What moves

  • Top (outside) face: every electronic part, placed by JLC in a single-sided Economic assembly. That covers the tuner, amp, charger, LDO, passives, the upright USB-C, both charge LEDs, and three JST sockets (battery, speaker, antenna).
  • Inside face: only the speaker (under the grille), the LiPo stacked on its back, the zip-tie slots, the CGW logo and text, and the solid ground ring under the band.
  • Plug-through holes: each lead comes up through its own hole beside its socket and plugs in on top. The battery and speaker leads come from inside the jar. The antenna wire can drop back inside through its hole, or run outside across the lid.
  • Volume/power pot: on top, fitted by JLC if a stocked switched pot exists (Q-T2), otherwise hand-soldered as before.
  • Whip: the plated M3 mount stays on top, on the same connection as the antenna socket.

Why it's better

  • One-sided assembly again: Economic PCBA (~$8 setup + 1 stencil) instead of two-sided Standard (~$25 + 2 stencils). That saves roughly $20–30 per order.
  • Ordinary top-side LEDs replace the reverse-mount parts. A red Basic part (C2286) and a green Basic/Extended part are about $0.01 each instead of $0.04–0.07, and it drops one or two Extended fees.
  • Right-angle sockets we already have numbers for: JST-PH S2B-PH-SM4-TB (C295747) and the XH G-Switch (C23067528). The vertical PH socket is no longer needed.
  • More room for parts. On top they only have to avoid the Ø36 grille, not the whole Ø49 speaker body, so the parts area grows by about 20%. Only through-holes (pot pins, plug holes, USB shell tabs, whip screw) must stay clear of the speaker and battery underneath.
  • Easy to probe and rework: every part is reachable without opening the jar.

The two parts you asked about

Speaker: FUET FS40MS0810-H5.0 (C482313)

  • A 40 mm, 5 mm-thin "micro" speaker, 8 Ω, 1 W, rated 92 dB, resonance 600 Hz, about $0.55 at 200+.
  • Sound: fine for talk radio and voices. Music will sound thin, because almost nothing below roughly 500–600 Hz. The GSPK4935 (45 mm driver in its own box, 95 dB) should have a little more body and be louder. Neither will thump. The sealed jar behind the thin speaker helps a bit.
  • Power: 1 W rated, so it's OK on battery (≈0.6 W). On USB the amp can reach ≈1.2 W, so I'd lower the amp gain slightly (R8 220k → 180k) so full volume doesn't overdrive it.
  • Big plus, space: at 5 mm thick it frees the inside of the jar. The LiPo could even lie flat beside it.
  • Need: its datasheet (or a photo) to see how it connects: solder tabs, bare wires, or a plug. That decides the socket.
  • Suggestion: buy 2–3 of each and listen in a jar before choosing. They're under a dollar.

Pot: Alps RK097111080J (C97444) won't work as-is

  • It's a 9 mm Alps RK097 through-hole pot (not SMD), 10 kΩ, and from the sibling part numbers it appears to be the horizontal version (shaft parallel to the board) without a switch. I couldn't open its datasheet from here, so please confirm.
  • No switch: it can't turn the radio on and off.
  • Horizontal: the shaft would point sideways across the lid, not up.
  • 10 kΩ: too low a load for the tuner output (minimum 10 kΩ, and the amp input loads it further). It would work with an extra 10 kΩ series resistor and a little more amp gain.
  • What to look for at JLC: a vertical pot with a switch, 20–100 kΩ. Search "potentiometer with switch vertical", "RK0971221" or "RK09D117" (Alps series with switch), "RV09 switch", "R0902N switch".
  • Fallback, fully JLC-placed: a vertical pot without switch (many stocked) plus a separate JLC-placed slide or push switch for power. That's 2 parts and a second control on the lid. Or keep the RV097NS and hand-solder it.

Open questions for this revision

  1. Q-T1 · Visible parts on the lid. All the chips will show on the outside. Keep the bare "circuit-board lid" look, or plan a cover (smoked acrylic or wood disc under the band, with holes for knob, USB and sockets)?
    Bare board for the prototypes. Decide on a cover after seeing them. A cover changes nothing on the PCB except the logos, which would move onto it.
  2. Q-T2 · A pot JLC can place. Please search LCSC for RV097NS, potentiometer with switch 50K and R0902N switch. I need a vertical PCB-mount part, ~50 kΩ, with an SPST switch and a ≈15 mm shaft, in stock at JLC. A or B taper both work.
    If none is stocked: hand-solder (7 joints per board) or consign your RV097NS pots to JLC's through-hole service.
  3. Q-T3 · Speaker plug. OK to put an XH plug on the GSPK4935 lead? Your choices: crimp it yourself (about $0.03 a plug plus a crimper), buy XH pigtails and splice, or ask INGHAi/LCSC for an XH termination at MOQ.
    Ask the factory first at 100+. For the prototypes, cut the 2.0 mm plug off and crimp an XH plug.
  4. Q-T4 · Socket direction. Right-angle sockets with their openings facing the holes, so leads come up and plug in sideways and stay flat? Or vertical sockets?
    Right-angle: both part numbers are already known, and they keep cables flat under a possible cover.
  5. Q-T5 · Hole size and finish. The plug holes (~7 × 5.5 and ~8.5 × 6.5 mm) are open slots in the lid. OK as plain routed slots, or should their edges be plated or rounded?
    Plain routed slots with 1 mm corner radii (JLC's minimum). They're free and won't cut the wires.
  6. Q-T6 · LEDs. Switch from reverse-mount to ordinary top-side 0603 LEDs (red C2286 Basic, plus a green Basic part)?
    Yes: cheaper, and up to two fewer Extended fees.
  7. Q-T7 · Logos. With parts on top, KOPN fits at about 12–13 mm tall beside the grille. The inside face is now free, so CGW, the project name and the battery warning go there large.
    If you want KOPN bigger, the grille could shrink from Ø36 to Ø32 (speaker still breathes fine) to free room.
  8. Q-T8 · Battery placement. Keep the LiPo stacked on the speaker's back, with two zip ties around both?
    Yes. The inside face has no space for it to lie flat beside the Ø49 speaker.
  9. Q-T9 · Speaker ear. Bolt the GSPK4935's ear to the board too? I need the ear hole's position and size.
  10. Still open from before: jar measurements (Q1), RV097NS pin layout (Q2) if it's hand-placed, and LCSC numbers for XC61CN2702MR, a ≤1 pF ESD diode (SOD-882), the 56 nH LQW18AN56NJ00D, CL10A475KO8NNNC and 0603WAF1000T5E. The vertical PH socket is no longer needed.
