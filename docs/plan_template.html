KOPN 89.5 Jar Radio

A single-station FM radio whose circuit board is the lid of a wide-mouth mason jar. It has no firmware, charges from USB-C, runs from USB alone or an optional LiPo, and has a wire or whip antenna. Board rev A is generated: schematic, layout, routing, and JLCPCB files. It is a first prototype. A few dimensions are still assumptions until the jar and parts are measured, and those are called out below.

Before you click "order"

  1. Measure the jar (Q1). If the lid isn't ~85.5 mm, I change one number and rebuild.
  2. Measure a real RV097NS pot (Q2). Its footprint is my best guess.
  3. Fill in the missing LCSC numbers (section 09).
  4. In JLC's assembly preview, check the rotation of each IC (pin-1 dots). Rotations from KiCad sometimes need a 90°/180° fix.
  5. Open the board in KiCad and look it over. Autorouted, so tidy-ups are welcome.

02Mechanical layout

Inside the jar

  • Speaker (Ø49 × 35 mm cavity) sits face-down under the grille, left of centre. Point its mounting ear toward the antenna-exit side (12 o'clock): there's free board there, and anywhere else the ear would hit parts or the glass.
  • Battery lies flat on the back of the speaker with a Kapton/foam pad. Two 200 mm zip ties go down through the slots above and below the speaker, around the speaker and battery stack, and back up. That one strap set holds both.
  • The battery lead plugs straight up into the vertical PH socket. The antenna lead plugs into the XH socket; spiral it inside, or feed it out the 3 mm hole.
  • Parts stay inside Ø72 (jar mouth ≈ Ø76). Outside Ø73 is solid ground on both sides, solder-masked, with stitching vias and no tracks.

04Antenna

Wire (included)

  • One wire, 75 cm, on XH pin 1. Leave pin 2 (ground) empty. A second wire twisted alongside would partly cancel the signal. Pulled apart into a "T", two 75 cm wires make a dipole, but that's a fringe-area upgrade, not the default.
  • Prototypes: buy 2-pin XH single-end leads (longest offered, usually 50 cm), pull the black wire's pin out of the housing with a needle, and solder or crimp 25 cm more onto the red. XH leads up to 50 cm (eBay) · Tayda XH 30 cm.
  • Volume: order custom leads from an AliExpress harness shop. Search "custom JST XH 2.54 cable assembly" and ask for: XH 2-pin housing, 1 position populated, 750 mm 26 AWG stranded (silicone if offered), far end stripped and tinned. Typically $0.10–0.30 each at 100+.

Telescopic whip (optional)

  • Screws into the plated M3 hole on top, same connection as the wire. Use one or the other.
  • Buy M3 male thread, 7-section, ~75 cm extended: 5× M3 male 7-section AM/FM whips (eBay) · Silicon Chip 750 mm, 3 mm screw hole (female; needs an M3 screw from below) · uxcell 111 cm, 4 pc (longer than needed).
  • Expected reception in Columbia: the coiled internal wire is fine above ground; trailing wire or whip for basements and the far west side.

05Speaker and battery

Speaker: INGHAi GSPK4935PN-8R2W-L270 (LCSC C530575)

  • Ø49 × 35 mm enclosed cavity speaker with a Ø45 driver, 8 Ω, 2 W, 95 dB (loud for its size), PET cone, 270 mm leads on a 2.0 mm 2-pin plug, plus a mounting ear. $0.79 at 10+, $0.75 at 500+.
  • Being sealed, it sounds the same whatever the jar does. That's a good choice.
  • Cut the plug off and solder the leads to the SPK+/SPK− pads. Its 2.0 mm plug would also fit the battery socket, and swapping them could damage the amp.
  • The cost of using it: at Ø49 it takes most of the inside face, so parts are packed tightly into the crescent beside it. A generic Ø40 speaker (~$0.27) frees about 40% more room, if rev B needs it.

Battery: 500 mAh protected LiPo, PH2.0 (eBay, $2.83 each in 100s)

  • Size must be ≤ 30 × 35 mm (it sits on the Ø49 speaker back); 6 × 25 × 30 mm (602530) is ideal.
  • Check polarity on every batch before plugging in. The socket has + and − printed beside it.
  • Cheaper at volume: factory-direct 502530/602530 cells run ~$0.70–1.20 with a PH lead (e.g. Liter Energy 602530). Ask for the UN38.3 report; shipping needs it.
  • Runtime: ~5–8 h at moderate volume (~60–90 mA). Charge time ~1.5 h at 400 mA.

08Buy, solder, assemble

1 · Buy

  • JLCPCB: PCB + inside-face assembly: upload jar-radio-gerbers.zip, then BOM.csv + CPL.csv. Options: 2 layers, 1.6 mm, black mask, white silk, HASL lead-free, "Economic" PCBA, assemble bottom side.
  • LCSC (same cart): USB-C C5156600 ×1/radio, GSPK4935 speaker C530575 ×1/radio.
  • eBay/AliExpress: RV097NS B50K pot + knob kit (your $23/20), protected 500 mAh LiPo (your $283/100), XH leads, 200 mm zip ties, Kapton tape; optional M3 whips.
  • Local: wide-mouth 8 oz jars with bands (~$0.90).

2 · JLC assembles (inside face)

Tuner, crystal, station and band resistors, reset detector, antenna ESD/1 nF/56 nH, NS8002 amp, TP4056 charger, load-share FET and diode, LDO, both reverse-mount LEDs, PH and XH sockets, and all passives.

3 · You solder

  1. Volume pot: 5 pins + 2 tabs, through-hole, body on top.
  2. USB-C: 6 small SMD pads + 2 shell tabs on top; flux and a fine tip.
  3. Speaker leads: cut the plug, 2 through-hole pads (SPK+ red, SPK− black).
  4. Calibration (one prototype): remove R2 + R3, fit a 100 kΩ trimmer in RV2 (1 = TUNE1, 2 = wiper, 3 = bottom), tune to KOPN, measure both halves, choose the final pair.

4 · Assemble

  1. Speaker face-down under the grille, ear toward the antenna-exit side.
  2. Pad + battery on the speaker back, two zip ties through the four slots.
  3. Plug in the battery (polarity checked) and the antenna lead.
  4. Knob on, lid on the jar, band on.

09Open questions and lookups

  1. Jar measurements: flat lid OD, band lip inner diameter, band inside height, jar mouth ID, inside depth, and how far the band turns on the stock lid.
    Board OD and keep-outs are one number each in gen_pcb.py; I'll rebuild once you send them.
  2. RV097NS pot, measured: pin pitch, distance from shaft centre to the 3-pin row and to the 2 switch pins, positions of the 2 support legs.
    The current footprint assumes 2.5 mm pitch, rows 6.5 mm either side of the shaft, legs at ±5 mm. Oversized holes absorb small errors, but please measure before ordering.
  3. Speaker ear: distance from speaker centre to the ear hole, and the hole size.
    Optional: I can add a screw hole so the ear bolts down as well as being zip-tied.
  4. Battery you bought: exact L × W × T and which wire colour is on which plug pin.
